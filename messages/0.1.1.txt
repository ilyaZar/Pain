v0.1.1

This update adds safer resize behavior for complex layouts.

- greedy_pane can push neighboring separators when a resize reaches them
- minimum_pane_size prevents panes from shrinking below a configured percent
- greedy resizes are cancelled when the resulting layout cannot keep every
  pane above the minimum

The default `minimum_pane_size=10` keeps panes that have been shrunk still
visible.

For details, see https://github.com/ilyaZar/Pain/releases/tag/v0.1.1
